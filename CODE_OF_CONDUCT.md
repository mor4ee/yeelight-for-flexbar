# Code of Conduct

This project is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
environment.

Contributors are expected to:

- discuss ideas and disagreements constructively;
- focus feedback on behavior and technical decisions;
- respect privacy and avoid publishing personal or network-sensitive data;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or deliberately disruptive
  behavior.

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when
these expectations are violated. Conduct concerns should be reported
privately through the repository's Security or maintainer contact channels.
